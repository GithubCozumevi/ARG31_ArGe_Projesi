Genel görünüm

Diversity · distinct-1 and distinct-2

Raw numbers mislead; equal sample size is required to compare

distinct-1 reports how many of all the words in a category are distinct from one another. If 100 words were written in total and 40 of them are different, distinct-1 = 0.40. As the number approaches 1, the model is picking a different word each time; as it approaches 0, it keeps returning the same few words.

distinct-2 runs the same calculation over adjacent word pairs instead of single words ("for office", "office supplies"). When the words differ but the ordering stays the same, only the pairwise measure catches it: "general expense, I bought" and "general expense, I paid" look fine under distinct-1 but the repetition resurfaces under distinct-2.

This ratio is sensitive to sample size: as a set grows, finding a new word gets harder and the ratio falls on its own. Comparing a 12,000-text category with a 2,000-text one as-is handicaps the larger one. For that reason the measurement on the left shows all texts, while the one on the right shows every category downsampled to the same size as the smallest one ( texts).

The ranking reverses. Looking at all texts, manipulative appears the most diverse; at equal sample size adequate takes the lead and AI-generated drops to the bottom as the most memorizable category. The formal register confines it to a narrow set of fixed patterns.
